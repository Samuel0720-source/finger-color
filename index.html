How it works

01

Find the fingertips

A hand-tracking model (MediaPipe) finds 21 points on your hand in every frame and places a small box on each fingertip and on your palm.

02

Measure the color

Each box is converted to CIELAB, a color space built to match human vision. The palm is used as a reference so changes in room light cancel out.

03

Time the recovery

The change from your saved normal color is smoothed over 10 seconds. Recovery is the moment it stays below your own noise level for 30 seconds.

Privacy

All video processing happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Your normal color is stored only in this browser.

Not a medical device

This is a personal research tool built as a student project. It does not diagnose any condition. Share your report with a clinician.
