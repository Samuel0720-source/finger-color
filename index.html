Should you see a doctor about your hands?

Answer questions taken from published clinical criteria, let your camera measure what it can, and see how your hand compares with the hallmark features of three common conditions. You get a clear next step and a report to bring to your appointment. It is not a diagnosis.

Movement test

Palm toward the camera, whole hand in view. Hold it open for the countdown, then slowly make a fist and open it all the way, 10 times. The test stops by itself.

Start the camera first.

Stop if it hurts and never force a locked finger. A catch is a pause in mid-bend followed by a quick snap open.

Thumb reach test

Palm toward the camera. After the countdown, touch your thumb tip to the tip of your index, middle, ring and little finger, in that order. You have 6 seconds per finger.

Start the camera first.

The muscles that move the thumb across the palm are powered by the nerve that carpal tunnel squeezes. This checks reach, not strength.

How the check works

Each condition has a list of hallmark features from clinical criteria. The app marks each feature as present, absent or not checked, using your answers, guided tests and the camera, then applies the criteria to suggest a next step.

Raynaud's

Uses the 3-step international consensus criteria: cold sensitivity, a white and blue color change, and at least 3 of 7 supporting features. The camera can supply four of them: the color phases, a photo of an episode, a sharp border, and the red phase on rewarming. It also measures how many fingers change and how long they take to recover.

See a doctor soon if it started after 40, affects one side, comes with fingertip sores or symptoms of autoimmune disease. Get care today if a finger turns black or stays white.

Trigger finger

Places your finger on the Green scale. Grade I is a tender spot at the base of the finger; II is catching you can straighten yourself; III needs your other hand or cannot bend fully; IV stays bent. The camera measures catches and how straight and how bent each joint gets, including the thumb.

Get care today for a red, hot, swollen finger, which can be a tendon infection.

Carpal tunnel

Uses the CTS-6: numbness in the thumb to ring finger (3.5 points), waking at night (4), thumb muscle weakness (5), Phalen test (5), two-point discrimination (4.5) and Tinel sign (4). A score of 12 or more out of 26 means carpal tunnel is likely. The camera adds a thumb reach test.

See a doctor soon for numbness that never goes away or a wasted thumb muscle.

Sources and limits
  • Maverakis E, et al. International consensus criteria for the diagnosis of Raynaud's phenomenon. J Autoimmun. 2014. PubMed
  • Raynaud phenomenon. Merck Manual Professional Edition. merckmanuals.com
  • Trigger finger: evaluation, management and outcomes (Green classification). Surgery Open Science / SurgiColl
  • Graham B, et al. The CTS-6. Carpal Tunnel Syndrome 6 calculator. MDCalc
  • Carpal tunnel syndrome referral criteria. NHS Scotland. Right Decisions

The criteria were written for clinicians examining a patient. Here you answer and test yourself, which is less reliable. Camera features use engineering thresholds checked only on synthetic signals so far, not on patients. The app never calculates a probability of disease.

Privacy

Video is analyzed on your device and never uploaded. Your answers stay in this browser tab and are cleared when you close it. Measurement history is stored only in this browser.

Not a medical device

A student research project. It does not diagnose any condition or replace a doctor. If you are worried about a symptom, seek medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
