Finger catching test

Hold your hand up with the palm facing the camera. Slowly make a fist, then open it all the way. Repeat 10 times. The test stops by itself.

Start the camera first.

Why: a finger that stops partway and then snaps straight is called catching. It can be a sign of trigger finger. The app measures the middle joint angle of each finger from the 3D hand points.

Thumb tapping test

Hold your hand side-on to the camera. After the countdown, tap your thumb and index finger together as fast and as wide as you can for 10 seconds.

Start the camera first.

Why: thumb speed and rhythm are simple measures of hand dexterity, which can drop when the nerve to the thumb is squeezed (as in carpal tunnel syndrome). This test does not diagnose anything.

How it works

01

Find the hand

A hand-tracking model (MediaPipe) finds 21 points on your hand in every video frame, both on the picture and in 3D.

02

Measure

Color: each fingertip is converted to CIELAB and compared with your palm. Movement: joint angles and the thumb-to-finger gap come from the 3D points, so camera distance does not matter.

03

Score

Recovery time after cold, catches (a finger that stops, then snaps open), and taps per second with rhythm steadiness. Compare each result with your own history.

Privacy

All video processing happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Your normal color is stored only in this browser.

Not a medical device

This is a personal research tool built as a student project. It does not diagnose any condition. Share your report with a clinician.
