Finger catching

Palm toward the camera, whole hand in view. Slowly make a fist, then open it all the way. Repeat 10 times. The test stops by itself.

Start the camera first.

A finger that stops partway and then snaps straight is called catching. It can be a sign of trigger finger. The app tracks the middle joint of each finger in 3D.

Thumb tapping

Hold your hand side-on to the camera. After a 3-second countdown, tap your thumb and index finger together, fast and wide, for 10 seconds.

Start the camera first.

Tapping speed and rhythm are simple measures of hand dexterity, which can drop when the nerve to the thumb is squeezed, as in carpal tunnel syndrome. This test does not diagnose anything.

How it works

  1. Find the hand

    A hand-tracking model (Google MediaPipe) finds 21 points on your hand in every video frame, both on the picture and in 3D.

  2. Measure

    Color is converted to CIELAB, a scale built around human vision, and compared with your palm so room light cancels out. Joint angles and the thumb-to-finger gap come from the 3D points, so distance from the camera does not matter.

  3. Score

    Recovery time after cold, the number of catches, and taps per second with rhythm steadiness. Each result is compared with your own history, not with other people.

Privacy

Video is analyzed on your device and never uploaded. Your normal color and history are stored only in this browser. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Not a medical device

This is a personal research tool built as a student project. It does not diagnose any condition. Share your report with a clinician if something worries you.
